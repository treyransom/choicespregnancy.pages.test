Self-Check

Could I Be
Pregnant?

This quick self-assessment can help you understand your symptoms. It is not a medical diagnosis.

Question 1 of 8

Have you been sexually active in the last 3 months?

Question 2 of 8

Have you missed a period or is it late?

Question 3 of 8

Were you using birth control consistently?

Question 4 of 8

Are you experiencing nausea or morning sickness?

Question 5 of 8

Have you noticed breast tenderness, swelling, or changes?

Question 6 of 8

Are you feeling unusually tired or fatigued?

Question 7 of 8

Have you experienced food aversions, unusual cravings, or sensitivity to smells?

Question 8 of 8

Are you experiencing frequent urination, mood swings, or light spotting?

This quiz is not a medical diagnosis. Only a pregnancy test can confirm pregnancy. All our tests are free and confidential.
