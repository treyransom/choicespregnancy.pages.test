About Us

A Safe Place for
Answers & Support

Choices Pregnancy Care Center has served Gainesville and Hall County for over 15 years. We believe every woman deserves quality care and accurate information, regardless of her circumstances.

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, all our services are completely free. We are funded by generous donations from individuals, churches, and businesses in our community. We are HIPAA compliant and all visits are confidential.

Our Mission

What We
Believe
