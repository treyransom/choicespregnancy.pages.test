FAQs: STI Testing

We know getting tested can feel overwhelming. Here are answers to the questions we hear most.

The testing process itself is quick -- usually just a few minutes. The entire appointment, including a private consultation, typically takes about 30 minutes. Results are usually available within a few days, and we will follow up with you personally.

The CDC recommends that all sexually active women under 25 get tested for chlamydia and gonorrhea every year. You should also get tested anytime you have a new sexual partner, if a partner has tested positive for an STI, or if you experience any symptoms. Since most STIs have no symptoms, regular testing is the best way to stay on top of your health.

We will make sure you understand your options and feel supported every step of the way.

Untreated STIs during pregnancy can cause serious complications for both you and your baby, including premature birth, low birth weight, and infections passed to the newborn. We offer free STI testing specifically for pregnant moms as part of our pregnancy care services.
