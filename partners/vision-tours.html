A One-Hour Visit

Numbers Tell Part of the Story.
A Tour Tells the Rest.

We often talk about impact at Choices — lives saved, families supported, futures rewritten. A Vision Tour invites you to step into that story. It’s a one-hour guided visit to our center in Gainesville, where you’ll walk through the same doors our patients do and see the mission up close.

What to Expect

During your one-hour Vision Tour, you’ll:

  • Step inside our Gainesville center and get a firsthand look at where care happens.
  • Walk through the patient experience and gain a better understanding of what a woman may be facing when she comes to Choices.
  • Meet members of our team and hear more about the work they do every day.
  • Learn about our programs and services, and how we come alongside women and men in our community.
  • Discover opportunities to serve and ways to use your time, talents, and passions to get involved.

You’ll leave with a deeper understanding of Choices, the women we serve, and the many ways you can be part of the mission.
