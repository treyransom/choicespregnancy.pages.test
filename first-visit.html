First Visit

What to Expect at
Your First Appointment

Nervous about coming in? That's completely normal. Here's exactly what happens from the moment you arrive.

1

Arrive & Check In

Walk in or arrive for your appointment. No paperwork pile—just a simple, private intake form. No ID or insurance needed.

2

Private Consultation

Meet one-on-one with a caring advocate in a private room. Share what's going on and ask any questions. Everything is confidential.

3

Pregnancy Test

If needed, take a free lab-quality pregnancy test. Results in under 10 minutes while you wait.

4

Ultrasound (if applicable)

If your test is positive, you may be offered a free limited ultrasound performed by a certified sonographer.

5

Information & Resources

Your advocate will provide accurate information about all your options and connect you with resources and support.

6

Follow-Up Plan

Before you leave, we'll discuss next steps and schedule any follow-up visits. You're never alone in this.

What to Bring

Nothing! Just yourself. No insurance, ID, or payment needed. Bring a support person if you'd like.
