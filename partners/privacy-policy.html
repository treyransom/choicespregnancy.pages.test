Choices Pregnancy Care Center ("Choices") respects your privacy. This page describes how information is handled on our partners website.

Information We Collect

If you sign up for our newsletter, volunteer, or contact us through a form, we collect the information you provide (such as your name, email address, and phone number) so we can respond and keep you updated.

Donations

Online donations are processed by third-party providers (such as EasyTithe and Amplify). Payment details are entered on and handled by those services, not on this website.

How We Use Information

We use your information only to communicate with you about Choices, our events, and ways to help. We do not sell or rent your information.
