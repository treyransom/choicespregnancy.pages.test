Who We Are

Our Mission

Choices Pregnancy Care Center is a faith-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit serving Gainesville and Hall County for over 15 years. We provide free, confidential medical services, education, and support for women facing unplanned pregnancies.

As a licensed medical facility staffed by trained professionals, we offer pregnancy testing, limited obstetric ultrasounds, options counseling, STI testing, and our Bright Course parenting education program — all at no cost.

Our Beliefs

Statement of Faith

We believe in the sanctity of human life from conception to natural death. We are motivated by the love of Jesus Christ to serve all women with compassion, dignity, and respect — regardless of faith, background, or circumstances.

We are affiliated with NIFLA, Heartbeat International, and Care Net — organizations that share our commitment to providing compassionate, life-affirming care.
