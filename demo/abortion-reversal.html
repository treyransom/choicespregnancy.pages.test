Chemical Abortion
Reversal Referral

If you've taken the first dose of the abortion pill (mifepristone) and have changed your mind, there may still be time. Act quickly.

How It Works

The chemical abortion process involves two medications taken 24-48 hours apart. The first pill, mifepristone, blocks progesterone, the hormone needed to sustain a pregnancy. The second pill, misoprostol, causes the uterus to contract and expel the pregnancy.

If you've only taken the first pill (mifepristone) and regret your decision, the Abortion Pill Rescue Network may be able to help. The reversal process involves administering progesterone to counteract the effects of mifepristone.

Time-sensitive: The sooner you act, the better the chances. Do not wait.

What We Do

Referral Services

We provide referrals to the Abortion Pill Rescue Network, connecting you with medical professionals who specialize in this process.

We Do Not Perform Reversals

We do NOT perform the reversal procedure ourselves. We connect you with the medical professionals who can help.

Medical Connections

We connect you with doctors and healthcare providers experienced in the abortion pill reversal protocol.

Emotional Support

We offer emotional support and counseling throughout the process, no matter the outcome.

This Is Time-Sensitive

Call immediately if you've changed your mind after taking the first abortion pill. The sooner you act, the better the chances.
