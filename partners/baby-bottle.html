How It Works

Fill a Bottle,
Change a Life

Churches, businesses, and individuals can participate in our baby bottle fundraiser. Pick up bottles at our office and fill them with coins, cash, or checks — or donate virtually online.

The Baby Bottle Campaign is one of our most popular community initiatives, engaging people of all ages in supporting our mission. It's a simple, fun way to make a big impact.
