How It Works

Bright Course is an interactive parenting education program. Attend classes on topics like prenatal health, infant care, and budgeting. Each class earns you credits to "shop" in our Baby Boutique for items like diapers, clothes, car seats, and more.

🎓 Free Classes 🎁 Earn Baby Items 👥 Community Support 📚 Expert-Led

What You'll Learn

🍎 Prenatal Health & Nutrition

What to eat, what to avoid, and how to have a healthy pregnancy.

👶 Newborn Care Basics

Feeding, bathing, sleep safety, and bonding with your baby.

💰 Budgeting for Baby

Financial planning, community resources, and making your money stretch.

🩺 Infant CPR & Safety

Life-saving skills and how to baby-proof your home.

🤱 Breastfeeding 101

Techniques, troubleshooting, and support for nursing moms.

💚 Emotional Wellness

Managing stress, postpartum health, and self-care for new moms.

What You Can Earn

Each class earns credits to shop in our Baby Boutique:

Diapers & Wipes Baby Clothes Car Seats Cribs Bottles & Formula Strollers Blankets & Toys
