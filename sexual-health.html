Sexual Health

Free, Confidential
STI Testing

STIs are more common than you think. Getting tested is quick, confidential, and completely free at Choices.

Why Get Tested?

Many STIs have no visible symptoms. You could have an infection and not know it. Left untreated, STIs can cause serious health problems including infertility, chronic pain, and complications during pregnancy. Testing is the only way to know your status.

Chlamydia

  • Most common bacterial STI in the U.S.
  • Often has NO symptoms - you may not know
  • Easily curable with antibiotics
  • Can cause infertility if untreated
Get Tested Free

Gonorrhea

  • Rates rising fast, especially ages 15-24
  • Some strains becoming drug-resistant
  • Treatable - early detection matters
  • Can spread to other parts of the body
Get Tested Free

HPV

  • Most common STI - nearly everyone gets it
  • Vaccine available and highly effective
  • Linked to cervical and other cancers
  • Many strains clear on their own
Get Tested Free

HIV & Herpes

  • Lifelong but manageable with treatment
  • Many people live full, healthy lives
  • Testing is the key first step
  • Early treatment greatly improves outcomes
Get Tested Free
