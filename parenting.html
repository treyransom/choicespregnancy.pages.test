Earn While You Learn

Our Bright Course program lets you take online classes on prenatal health, infant care, child development, budgeting, and healthy relationships. As you complete lessons, you earn credits to shop in our Baby Boutique for diapers, clothes, car seats, and more -- all completely free.

Explore Bright Course

Community Resources

We can help connect you with local resources to support you on your parenting journey:

  • Housing assistance -- Programs to help you find safe, affordable housing
  • Childcare referrals -- Options for affordable or subsidized childcare
  • Job training programs -- Resources to build skills and find employment
  • Support groups -- Connect with other moms who understand your journey
  • Government programs -- Help applying for WIC, Medicaid, TANF, and other assistance

You're Not Alone
