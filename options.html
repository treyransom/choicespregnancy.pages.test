Your Choices

What Are
My Options?

You have options, and we're here to help you understand each one without pressure or judgment. This is your decision, and we respect that.

👶

Parenting

Many women who feel unprepared discover they have more support than they realized. We can help you explore resources, create a plan, and connect with community support.

  • Financial assistance programs (WIC, Medicaid, TANF)
  • Parenting classes through Bright Course
  • Free baby supplies and resources
  • Ongoing support network and mentoring
  • Housing and childcare referrals

What We Provide

Our Bright Course program lets you earn baby essentials while learning parenting skills. We also connect you with community resources for financial assistance, housing, childcare, and more.

💝

Adoption

Adoption is a brave, loving choice. Today's adoption options give you more control than ever - you can choose the family and level of contact that feels right for you.

  • Open, semi-open, or closed adoption
  • You choose the adoptive family
  • Medical and living expenses covered
  • Counseling and support throughout
  • You can change your mind before finalization

Types of Adoption

Open: Ongoing contact with the adoptive family. Semi-open: Communication through an agency. Closed: No identifying information shared. We can connect you with licensed adoption agencies to explore what feels right.

Not Sure Where to Start?

That's okay. Our caring advocates will listen to your unique situation and help you think through your options - without pushing you in any direction.
