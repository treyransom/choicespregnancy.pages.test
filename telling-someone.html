How to Tell Someone
You're Pregnant

Whether it's a partner, parent, or friend—having the conversation can feel overwhelming. Here's some practical advice.

Telling Your Partner

  • - Choose a calm, private moment.
  • - Be direct—start with "I need to talk to you about something important."
  • - Share how you're feeling.
  • - Give them time to process.
  • - If you're worried about their reaction, tell someone you trust first or come talk to us.

Telling Your Parents

  • - Pick a time when they're not stressed or distracted.
  • - You might start with "I need your support with something."
  • - It's okay to write a letter if face-to-face feels too hard.
  • - Remember: their initial reaction may not be their lasting one.
  • - Bring a trusted friend or counselor for support if needed.

Telling a Friend

  • - Choose someone you trust to keep your confidence.
  • - Be clear about what kind of support you need.
  • - It's okay to say "I just need you to listen right now."

General Tips

  • - You don't have to tell everyone at once.
  • - It's okay to wait until you're ready.
  • - Practice what you want to say.
  • - You deserve support, not judgment.
  • - We can help you prepare—come talk to us first.

Need Help Having the Conversation?

Our advocates can help you think through what to say and even be there for support. You don't have to do this alone.
